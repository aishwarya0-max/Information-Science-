This blog is to traverse through the ideologies of what do you mean by Information. The first unit we covered was 'What is Information' which is one of the most intresting thing I came across. In this unit we mainly covered **"DIKW also known as the _Data Knowledge Wisdom model_"**. At begning this felt more intuitive as this depicts the linear flow of information that is raw data which is gradually transformed into complex structures. The simplicity made me understand the progression but tranisition from knowledge wisdom was often blurry. Data was raw facts which then organised to form Information, knowldge comes from identifying these patterns, whereas wisdom is making decision based on our understanding. 

However as I started applying and questioning whether this is applicable on everyday processes. I soon understood this isn't the case.
