Unit 1 : What is Information

This blog is to traverse through the ideologies of what do you mean by Information. The first unit we covered was _‘What is Information’_which is one of the most intresting thing I came across. In this unit we mainly covered “DIKW also known as the Data Knowledge Wisdom model”. At begning this felt more intuitive as this depicts the linear flow of information that is raw data which is gradually transformed into complex structures. The simplicity made me understand the progression but tranisition from knowledge wisdom was often blurry. Data was raw facts which then organised to form Information, knowldge comes from identifying these patterns, whereas wisdom is making decision based on our understanding.

UNIT 2 : Nature Of Image Transformation

Personally what stood out for me in this particular unit was that information problems are not only working with data but also making small manipulation to get a better understanding. Topic like image transformation involves a lot of choices which hugely impact the outcome.  Image manipulation was easier to connect as we had a template to work with and visualise flipping it or adjusting brightness by bringing changes in the pixels. This made me realise how information is represented and whether changes are reversible or associative.  Transformation of images was most easiest for me and also at the same time hard as it had lot of mathematical aspects which is required for it. But at the same with help of these tools it will make the life easier by applying them on function which act as real life filters by either increasing contrast, flipping the image, inverting the images. Personally I found optimisation to be interesting yet hard as it connected towards  finding the best solution but the methods were abstract. But I realised what is the “best” depends on how we choose the conditions. But these optimisation are something which wired into our brain that we regularly use in our day to day life. For example finding the best route based on our needs like least time, with more restaurants etc. Other would be budgeting were we set a value for each item to have a minimum amount to be spend.
