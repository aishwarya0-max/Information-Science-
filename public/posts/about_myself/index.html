Unit 1 : What is Information

This blog is to traverse through the ideologies of what do you mean by Information. The first unit we covered was _‘What is Information’_which is one of the most intresting thing I came across. In this unit we mainly covered “DIKW also known as the Data Knowledge Wisdom model”. At begning this felt more intuitive as this depicts the linear flow of information that is raw data which is gradually transformed into complex structures. The simplicity made me understand the progression but tranisition from knowledge wisdom was often blurry. Data was raw facts which then organised to form Information, knowldge comes from identifying these patterns, whereas wisdom is making decision based on our understanding. However as I started applying and questioning whether this is applicable on everyday processes. I soon understood this isn’t the case as knowledge does not simly emerge from filtering out the information or by breaking down the comple task into a organised structure. As most of time, learning is not linear. Most of the time knowledge influence how we intrepret data, therefore we cannot strictly say that it upward flow from data to wisdom.

I also found it quite amusing that this model is about simpler linear learning but in reality how we understand anything is much more complex which involves a lot processes like mistakes, intuition, intrepretation. In addition we know human has a limitation of how much information or how many variables which can be processed at a time. Therefore it is challenging if we put constraints or follow certain model or representation for process information or any data. Another point inorder make it more conveyable data visualisation plays an important role. Effective vislualisation makes patterns clear and help intrepretation of any data way easier if it it simplified. As we discussed in class this also wide spectrum where we dealt with a lot of complex graphs which made it hard for us to ever make a point what is being presented. On the other there were lot of graphs which were presented with the users o mind.

In my opinion by combining all of the ideas we covered in Unit 1, it is always important to keep in mind about the targeted audience while making anything. Making sense of data not always follow a linear attern it also means understanding it and also representing it which is understandable by human or the congnitive limit of us.
